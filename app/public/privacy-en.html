2. Scope and legal framework

This policy applies to website visitors, prospects, customers, suppliers and other individuals whose data SOMA Core processes through its website, email, meetings, proposals or business relationships.

Processing follows Article 15 of the Colombian Constitution, Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1074 of 2015 and applicable Colombian regulations.

3. Personal data we process

  • Identity and contact data, including name, email, phone, company, role and city.
  • Information contained in inquiries, forms, meetings, support requests, proposals and business documents.
  • Contract, billing and compliance data where a commercial relationship exists.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser, device, pages visited, timestamps, security logs and cookie choices.
  • Communication history and marketing preferences.

Data may come from you, your organization, your device or authorized third parties.

4. Processing purposes

  • Answer inquiries, requests, complaints, quotations and demo requests.
  • Prepare proposals and manage pre-contractual, contractual and post-contractual relationships.
  • Deliver, configure, support, secure and improve technology services.
  • Manage customers, suppliers, payments, invoicing, accounting and legal obligations.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, incidents and unauthorized access.
  • Measure website performance subject to cookie preferences.
  • Send commercial communications when authorized, with an opt-out at any time.
  • Comply with legal orders and establish or defend legal claims.

5. Authorization

Where required, SOMA Core obtains prior, express and informed authorization in writing, verbally, electronically or through unequivocal conduct. Processing without authorization occurs only in cases allowed by law.

6. Transfers and processing by third parties

Hosting, cloud, analytics, security, communications, support and accounting providers may process limited data under confidentiality and security duties.

International transfers or transmissions follow Law 1581 of 2012 and applicable safeguards. SOMA Core does not sell or rent personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for the stated purposes and applicable legal, accounting, contractual or defense periods. It is then securely deleted, anonymized or blocked unless retention is legally required.

8. Information security

SOMA Core applies reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures against loss, unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No internet-connected system offers absolute security. Report incidents to contacto@somacoretech.com.

9. Data subject rights

  • Know, access, update and correct your data.
  • Request evidence of authorization, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Know how your data has been used.
  • File a complaint with Colombia’s Superintendence of Industry and Commerce after completing the internal process where required.
  • Revoke authorization or request deletion when legally available.
  • Access processed data free of charge.

10. Inquiries and complaints

Email contacto@somacoretech.com with your identity, contact details, clear request and supporting documents.

Inquiries

We respond within ten (10) business days. If necessary, we will explain the delay and respond within five (5) additional business days.

Complaints

Incomplete complaints will receive a correction request within five (5) business days. Complete complaints are answered within fifteen (15) business days, extendable by eight (8) business days with prior explanation.

11. Minors and sensitive data

Business services are not directed to minors. We do not intentionally collect children’s data or request sensitive data through this website. Where a service requires sensitive data, specific notice and authorization will apply.

12. Cookies, automated decisions and changes

See our Cookie Policy. This public website does not make solely automated decisions with legal or similar significant effects.

We may update this policy for legal, operational or technical changes. Material changes will be communicated when required.
