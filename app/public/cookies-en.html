2. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data stored or accessed by a website to provide functions, remember preferences or measure use. Some belong to SOMA Core; others belong to service providers.

3. Cookies and technologies used

Necessary

These support operation, security and storage of your cookie choice. They cannot be disabled through the banner because they are essential to the requested function.

Analytics

With your consent, Vercel Analytics may measure visits and performance in aggregate. SOMA Core uses this information to understand stability and improve the website, not to sell personal profiles.

Specific retention periods may vary by technology and provider configuration. We review settings to limit information to the stated purpose.

4. Managing preferences

The banner lets you accept or reject analytics technologies. Your decision is stored locally and honored on future visits. You may also erase site data or block cookies through your browser; doing so may remove preferences or affect necessary functions.

Rejecting or withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. To change your choice, erase this website’s storage and reload the page.

Providers may process technical data from other countries under their terms and safeguards. See the Personal Data Processing Policy for purposes, transfers and your rights.
